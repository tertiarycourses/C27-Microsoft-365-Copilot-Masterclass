# Asteron Sharing and Sensitivity Simulation

All identities and settings below are synthetic. The default exercise is a tabletop review: record the requested control, owner and missing evidence without changing a real permission. Review only your lab copies; never inspect or modify real group membership unless the trainer explicitly authorises a sandbox path.

## Tabletop remediation record

1. Request restriction of the readiness workbook to the approved pilot-team group; record the control owner and evidence gap.
2. Exclude the participant contact list from the workflow.
3. Request verification of the department-heads group membership by the authorised owner.
4. Retain only aggregated cohort measures in the coordination pack.
5. Keep the external announcement on hold until privacy sign-off.
6. Keep the Outlook message unsent until its recipients and purpose are approved.

## Optional trainer-authorised sandbox

Only when the trainer explicitly authorises a sandbox may you perform and verify an actual permission change on the supplied lab copies. Record that authority, the changed control and the verification evidence. Do not use organisational files or real group membership.
