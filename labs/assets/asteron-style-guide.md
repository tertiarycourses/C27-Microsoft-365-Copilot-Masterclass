# Asteron Internal Style Guide

- Write in plain, direct business English.
- Prefer specific verbs and named owners.
- Use short paragraphs and parallel bullets.
- Use ISO dates (`yyyy-mm-dd`).
- Pair percentages with counts and denominators.
- Distinguish `Confirmed`, `Proposed` and `Open`.
- Do not turn an estimate, preference, proposal or question into a commitment.
- Avoid invented quotations, testimonials or visual evidence.
- Expand an acronym at first use.
- End with the action or decision required from the reader.
