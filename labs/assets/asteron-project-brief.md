# Asteron Client Portal Pilot — Project Brief

**Source ID:** SRC-01  
**Owner:** Mei Tan, Programme Lead

**Effective date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** Approved internal project brief

## Purpose

Asteron Services will run an internal client portal pilot to validate navigation, support handoffs and readiness before any wider client communication. The pilot is a controlled learning exercise; it is not a public launch.

## Scope

- Pilot launch: 2026-08-18
- Cohort: 20 internal participants
- Departments: Operations, Sales, Finance, Human Resources and Information Technology
- Pilot period: 2026-08-18 to 2026-08-28
- Intended audience for project artifacts: the pilot team and department heads

## Required launch work

- Prepare the participant FAQ before the pilot begins.
- Prepare the cohort data export for the readiness dashboard.
- Complete the privacy review before any external announcement.
- Track training completion, attendance and support demand.

## Working targets

At the time this brief was approved, the data export target was 2026-08-12. The project decision log governs later changes to owners, dates and decision status.

## Open item

A support clinic on 2026-08-19 at 14:00 has been proposed. Room availability and the final invitation have not been confirmed.

## Limitations

This brief does not contain final readiness results. It does not authorise an external announcement, confirm the support-clinic room or replace the project decision log.
